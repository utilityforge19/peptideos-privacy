PeptideOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

PeptideOS ("the App") is a scheduling, organizational, tracking, reminder, and calculation tool intended for research workflow management. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By using PeptideOS, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Google Account Information

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App may access:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Firebase Authentication user ID

This information is used only for:

  • Authenticating your account
  • Linking your cloud backup data to your account
  • Restoring your backup data across devices
  • Maintaining continuity of optional cloud-based features

PeptideOS does not sell, rent, or share your Google account information for advertising or marketing purposes.

2. App Data and Optional Cloud Backup

PeptideOS may store app-related data in Firebase cloud services when optional cloud backup features are used.

Examples may include:

  • Protocols
  • Schedules
  • Vial information
  • App settings
  • Organizational workflow data

Cloud backup is used only for:

  • Backup and restore functionality
  • Syncing your app data across your own devices

Cloud backup is optional and tied to your authenticated account. The App remains usable without creating an account or enabling cloud backup.

3. Purchases and Premium Features

PeptideOS uses Google Play Billing for premium purchases.

Premium entitlement is verified through Google Play Billing APIs and is not granted solely from cloud backup data.

The App may temporarily store local premium entitlement information on your device to support functionality and restore scenarios.

PeptideOS does not process, receive, or store payment card information directly.

All purchases are processed by Google Play.

4. Automatically Collected Technical Information

Firebase and Google services may automatically generate technical identifiers required for operation of the App and its cloud services.

Examples may include:

  • Firebase Installation ID
  • Firebase Authentication identifiers
  • Device or app instance identifiers required by Firebase services

These identifiers are used only for:

  • App functionality
  • Authentication
  • Cloud backup infrastructure
  • Security and service operation

5. Trial Integrity and Abuse Prevention

To help protect the integrity of the App's free trial and prevent repeated trial resets through uninstalling and reinstalling the application, PeptideOS may generate and store a one-way cryptographic hash derived from a device identifier available to the application.

The original device identifier is not transmitted or stored. A one-way cryptographic hash derived from the device identifier may be transmitted to and stored within Firebase services solely for trial integrity and abuse-prevention purposes.

This information is used solely to:

  • Verify free trial eligibility
  • Prevent free trial abuse
  • Maintain trial continuity across app reinstalls
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App's licensing system

This information is not used for:

  • Advertising
  • Marketing
  • Analytics
  • Profiling
  • Cross-app tracking

The hashed identifier may be securely stored using Firebase Cloud Firestore and related Firebase services solely to enforce free trial eligibility, maintain trial continuity where applicable, and prevent abuse of the App's licensing system.

If you choose to use optional cloud backup features and sign in to a Google account, trial information may also be associated with your account solely to maintain trial continuity across devices and prevent abuse of the free trial system.

All trial integrity data is transmitted using encrypted connections. PeptideOS does not sell, rent, or share this information with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

PeptideOS does NOT:

  • Sell personal information
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use AdMob
  • Use third-party analytics platforms
  • Use behavioral tracking SDKs
  • Use third-party advertising identifiers for marketing
  • Collect precise location data

The App does not include third-party advertising networks.

7. Data Sharing

Your information is not sold to third parties.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers solely to provide App functionality, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Functions
  • Firebase App Check

These services operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

8. Data Security

PeptideOS uses reasonable security measures to help protect your information.

Sensitive entitlement and authentication-related local data are excluded from Android Auto Backup where appropriate.

Data transmitted through Firebase and Google services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard security practices.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose whether to sign in with Google
  • Disable or avoid cloud backup features
  • Uninstall the App at any time
  • Remove local app data through Android system settings

You may also remove access through your Google account settings.

If you wish to request deletion of applicable cloud-stored data associated with your account or trial integrity records, you may contact the email address listed below.

10. Intended Audience

PeptideOS is intended for adult users and is not directed toward individuals under 18 years of age.

If you are under 18, please do not use the App.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
