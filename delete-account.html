PeptideOS Account & Data Deletion

In the App: open Settings and tap "Delete cloud backup & account". This permanently deletes your cloud backup and your PeptideOS sign-in account, and takes effect immediately. It is available on every plan, including after the free trial has ended.

Without the App: to request deletion of your PeptideOS cloud backup data and associated account information, email:

utilityforge19@proton.me

Please include the Google account email associated with your PeptideOS backup.

Requested account-linked cloud backup data will typically be deleted within 30 days.

Local app data stored only on your device can be removed by uninstalling PeptideOS or clearing app storage in Android settings.

Premium purchase records are handled by Google Play and are not controlled directly by PeptideOS.

A trial-integrity record (a one-way hashed device identifier and trial start date, with no email, name, or health data) is kept to prevent repeated free trials. You can ask for it to be deleted at the email above. See the Privacy Policy for details.
